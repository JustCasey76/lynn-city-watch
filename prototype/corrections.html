Accuracy & accountability

Corrections & Updates

This site is held to a strict standard: every factual claim must trace to an official public record. When something here is wrong, incomplete, or missing context, we want to fix it in the open.

How we handle facts

To keep things clear, we separate four different things — and label them wherever they appear:

  • Public-record facts — drawn directly from official documents, with a source link.
  • Plain-English summaries — our restatement of what a record says, to make it readable.
  • Data analysis — comparisons and totals we calculate from sourced numbers.
  • Opinion / editorial — clearly labeled as opinion, never presented as fact.

Where the public record is uncertain, incomplete, disputed, or missing, we say so plainly rather than guess. We do not speculate about anyone's motives.

Corrections policy

If any information on this website is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or missing important context, you may submit a correction request with supporting documentation. Verified corrections will be reviewed and updated publicly, and logged below.

✎ Submit a correction

Please include a link to (or copy of) the official record that supports the correction. Corrections backed by a verifiable public source are prioritized.

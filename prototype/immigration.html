Spending analysis

Immigration-Related Spending

Every item in the recorded 2024–2026 City Council votes that touches immigration, newcomers, refugees, or asylum — shown with its gross amount, its documented funding source, and the estimated net local (Lynn taxpayer) cost. Where the records do not identify a funding source, this page says so instead of guessing. Each item links to its official source minutes.

§
What this page covers — and what it does not. This reflects items the City Council recorded a vote on (grants, transfers, orders, and resolutions) in the meeting minutes for 2024–2026. It is not the entire city budget. If a department spent money from a budget line that did not require a separate council vote, it would not appear here. The full line-item city budget is not yet loaded into this database, so this page cannot yet claim to be a complete accounting of every immigration-related dollar — only of what the council votes show. Net local taxpayer cost is calculated only where a source document identifies that item's funding; the citywide revenue average is never applied to an individual item.

Sources: City Council agendas & minutes · Mayor's Budget · Audited financial statements. See something missing or a document that adds context? Submit a correction.

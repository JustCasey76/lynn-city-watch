Our standard

Aggressive on transparency. Disciplined on accuracy.

We don't call anyone "corrupt." We show measurable, sourced facts: how someone voted, whether they showed up, where the money went — and let residents judge.

  • Official records first, with a link on every claim.
  • Facts kept separate from opinion; estimates clearly labeled.
  • Missing information is flagged, not filled with guesses.
  • Public archive of every correction.

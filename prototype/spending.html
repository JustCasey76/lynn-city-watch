This page separates gross spending from documented outside funding where the records allow. The goal is to show the estimated net local taxpayer cost of a spending decision, not just the sticker price. When the records do not identify the exact funding source, the page says so instead of guessing.

    Methodology

    How we calculate net local cost

    For a specific spending item, net local taxpayer cost = gross cost − documented outside funding (grants, state aid, federal funds, enterprise funds, etc.). We calculate it only when a source document actually identifies that item's funding.

    • We do not subtract the citywide average from every expense — a specific program may be funded very differently from the city as a whole.
    • If the record doesn't show the funding source, we say "the net local taxpayer cost cannot be determined from the available records" rather than guess.
    • Estimates are labeled as estimates. Audited figures are labeled as audited.

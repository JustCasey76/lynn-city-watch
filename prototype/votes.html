Voting Record Database

Every recorded 2026 Lynn City Council vote, transcribed from the official meeting minutes. Search and filter by topic, money involved, and outcome. Each vote links to its source minutes, shows the spending and net local cost where documented, and can be expanded to show how each member voted.

§
Where the minutes record only a tally and attendance (not a member-by-member roll call), each member's position is derived from the tally and labeled as such on the vote. Members the minutes name individually (dissents, abstentions, "present") are shown exactly as recorded.
